DMK Airport & Transfer Logistics

Terminal Structure

Don Mueang uses two physically connected passenger terminals within walking distance inside the same building:

  • Terminal 1: All International departures and arrivals.
  • Terminal 2: All Domestic departures and arrivals across Thailand.

Free Shuttle Bus to Suvarnabhumi (BKK)

Airports of Thailand (AOT) runs a free shuttle bus for passengers connecting between DMK and BKK with a valid flight ticket:

  • DMK Pick-up Location: Terminal 1, Level 1, Gate 6.
  • Transit Time: 50–90+ minutes depending on Bangkok expressway traffic.
