NAIA Transfer Logistics

PAL & AirAsia Terminal Split

International flights for both Philippine Airlines and AirAsia depart from Terminal 1. All domestic flights for both carriers depart from Terminal 2.

Inter-Terminal Transit Times

Terminals 1, 2, and 3 are separated by active runway grounds. Ground transit via Grab or taxi typically takes:

  • Terminal 1 ↔ Terminal 2: 5–10 minutes
  • Terminal 1/2 ↔ Terminal 3: 30–60 minutes in traffic
