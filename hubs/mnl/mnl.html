Terminal Quick Summary

Terminal 1

⚠️ Landside Transfer Only

Primary Role: Legacy Foreign & Int'l Hub

Transit Note: Requires shuttle or taxi via public roads (no airside walkway).

Mainly PAL Int'l, AirAsia Int'l & major foreign carriers.

Terminal 2

💡 Strictly Domestic

Primary Role: Domestic Terminal

Transit Note: 10–15 mins from T1 via road shuttle or Grab/taxi.

Exclusively handles PAL Domestic & AirAsia Domestic flights.

Terminal 3

✓ Major Hub / Walkway

Primary Role: Cebu Pacific Hub & Major Int'l

Transit Note: Direct access via Runway Manila footbridge to Newport World.

Main hub for Cebu Pacific (Int'l/Dom) & key international airlines.

NAIA Transfer Logistics

PAL & AirAsia Terminal Split

International flights for both Philippine Airlines and AirAsia depart from Terminal 1. All domestic flights for both carriers depart from Terminal 2.

Inter-Terminal Transit Times

Terminals 1, 2, and 3 are separated by active runway grounds. Ground transit via Grab or taxi typically takes:

  • Terminal 1 ↔ Terminal 2: 5–10 minutes
  • Terminal 1/2 ↔ Terminal 3: 30–60 minutes in traffic
