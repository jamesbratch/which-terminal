Why Terminal Verification Matters in SEA

Unlike Western transit hubs with intra-terminal passenger movers, airports like Manila, Bangkok, and Kuala Lumpur require exiting security and transferring across busy municipal roads.

  • Physical Isolation: Getting to the wrong terminal in Manila or Bangkok can delay you 45–60 minutes in ground traffic.
  • Carrier Shifts: Budget airlines in Southeast Asia frequently shift terminals during schedule updates.
