About WhichTerminal.com

A high-speed, mobile-first utility built to eliminate airport connection anxiety.

Our Mission

Airport terminal reassignments happen frequently, often leaving travelers scrambling to figure out where their flight departs from. Most airport websites are slow, ad-heavy, and difficult to navigate on weak cellular connections.

WhichTerminal.com was created to solve one problem instantly: show travelers verified terminal mappings in under a second with zero ads, zero bloat, and full offline capability.

Key Features

  • Instant Search: Find your terminal by typing an airline name or two-letter IATA code (e.g., PR, 5J, Z2, QR).
  • Offline Capable: Built as a Progressive Web App (PWA) so it works even when airport cell reception drops.
  • Verified Schedules: Updated regularly to reflect official airport authority terminal reassignments and carrier relocations.
